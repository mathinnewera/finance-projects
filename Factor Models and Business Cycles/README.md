# Factor Models and Business Cycles

Two notebook exercises on factor-based return analysis: rolling Fama–French factor regressions and CAPM performance across business-cycle regimes.

## Notebooks

- [Rolling factor regression](rolling_factor_regression.ipynb): estimates rolling alpha and market, SMB, and HML exposures for a stock.
- [CAPM across business cycles](capm_business_cycle_analysis.ipynb): estimates CAPM results for small- and large-cap portfolios, compares recession and expansion periods, and applies significance tests.

The notebooks refer to external CSV inputs, including `crsp_roll.csv`, `ff_daily.csv`, `Portfolios_Formed_on_ME_Daily.csv`, `ff_daily(1).csv`, and `USREC.csv`. Those inputs are not included in this folder.
