# Risk Measurement and Drawdown Analysis

Exercises on tail-risk measures and the behavior of individual indices and a diversified portfolio during market declines.

## Notebooks

- [Value at Risk analysis](value_at_risk_analysis.ipynb): derives portfolio-return risk measures and discusses the subadditivity property of Gaussian VaR.
- [Drawdown and co-drawdown analysis](drawdown_and_co_drawdown_analysis.ipynb): compares historical and Gaussian VaR/expected shortfall, computes index and portfolio drawdowns, and examines co-drawdown periods and probabilities for the CSI 300 and Hang Seng Index.

The drawdown notebook refers to `Ind_CSI_HSI.csv`, which is not included in this folder.
