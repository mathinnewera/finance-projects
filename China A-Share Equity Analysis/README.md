# China A-Share Equity Analysis

Coursework exploring valuation, accounting fundamentals, and the persistence of firm performance in Chinese listed companies.

## Included work

- **Questions 1–2:** Compare Main Board and GEM Board firms using P/E, P/B, R&D-to-assets, firm age, profitability, and return statistics; examine historical median P/E ratios.
- **Question 3:** Track how many firms remain above annual median ROA and revenue growth measures over time, and compare year-over-year persistence.
- **Results report:** Summarizes the descriptive statistics and market analysis.
