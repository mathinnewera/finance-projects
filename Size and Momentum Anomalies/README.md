# Size and Momentum Anomalies

An empirical study of size- and past-return-sorted portfolios in Chinese equities, including portfolio returns and factor regressions.

## Files

- [Analysis notebook](size_and_momentum_analysis.ipynb): prepares monthly stock and factor data, forms portfolios, estimates returns, and evaluates size and short-horizon momentum/reversal patterns.
- [Results report](ResultsPDF.pdf): presents the analysis and reported findings.
- [Input data](Data/): monthly trading and factor workbooks.
- [Empirical results](<Empirical Results/>): output workbooks `2a.xlsx` and `2b.xlsx`.

The report cites `2a.xlsx` and `2b.xlsx`; those workbook names are preserved. The notebook also writes its portfolio results to workbooks with those names.
