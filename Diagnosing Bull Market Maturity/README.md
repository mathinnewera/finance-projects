# Diagnosing Bull Market Maturity

An indicator-based study of Chinese equity bull-market episodes, comparing the 2015 and 2021 markets with a more recent episode.

## Analysis

The notebook calculates and compares trading-value and margin-balance ratios, new equity-fund issuance, the CSI 300 equity risk premium, the dividend-yield spread over government bonds, and price changes in representative stocks. The report interprets these indicators to assess the market-cycle stage.
